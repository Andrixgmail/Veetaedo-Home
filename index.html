Good to know

Questions we hear often

Can I get a couch in a different colour?

Yes. We do all colours. Send us the piece you like and the colour you want, and we will confirm the fabric options with you on WhatsApp.

Can you make a couch to fit my room?

Yes. Our couches can be measured to your space. For example, our Bubble U shaped couch is 3.5 m by 2 m by 2 m as shown, and can be adjusted. Send us your wall measurements for a quote.

How does payment work?

We take a 70% deposit before work starts and the remaining 30% upon delivery.

Do you deliver?

Yes, the balance is paid when your couch is delivered. Message us with your area and we will confirm delivery details and costs.

Are the prices fixed?

Prices on this site are for each piece as shown. Changes in size, colour or configuration may change the price. Some pieces can also be bought separately, for example the Pumpkin L shape couch alone is R13 000 with cushions.

Can I see the couches in person?

Absolutely. Our showroom in Leeuwfontein Heights, Roodeplaat, Pretoria is open every day from 09:00 to 18:00.
